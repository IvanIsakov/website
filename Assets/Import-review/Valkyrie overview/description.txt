Founded in 2017 by Ivan Isakov, Kourosh Atefipour and Oliver Cleveley-Jones, Valkyrie Industries have been developing a wearable technology, that allows user to naturally interact with objects in VR: touch them, feel their weight and resistance. Since 2025, Ivan is acting as a CEO/CTO of Valkyrie Industries. As a CTO, Ivan is responsible for hardware technology directions, product management, science behind and user testing. As a CEO, Ivan is responsible for investment, sales & marketing.
