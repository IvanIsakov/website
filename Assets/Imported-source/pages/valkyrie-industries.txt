VALKYRIE INDUSTRIES
Founded in 2017 by Ivan Isakov, Kourosh Atefipour and Oliver Cleveley-Jones, Valkyrie Industries have been developing a wearable technology, that allows user to naturally interact with objects in VR: touch them, feel their weight and resistance. Since 2025, Ivan is acting as a CEO/CTO of Valkyrie Industries.
As a CTO, Ivan is responsible for hardware technology directions, product management, science behind and user testing. As a CEO, Ivan is responsible for investment, sales & marketing.
eir bands
Wearable Muscle Haptics for VR
Using electrical muscle stimulation (EMS) synchronised with user's actions and movements in VR.
The muscle haptic sensations are very consistent, they exerts a force on your muscle, train your muscle, and make your muscle tired. As some of our testers said: “This is the closest I’ve ever experienced to the feeling of resistance in VR” and “My arms are aching today from that workout! Got a proper session in there!”.
The work on this particular devices started in September 2021, with the pre-production samples ready in September 2022. Devices have been manufactured by Luxshare & Cosmo.

How Do eir bands Work? | One Wearable for Strength, Recovery & Muscle Relief | EMS Technology | VR
Discover eir bands — the EMS muscle stimulator designed to strengthen muscles, accelerate recovery, and support pain relief.
One wearable. Multiple uses. Train smarter at home or take your workouts further with immersive VR fitness.
Using the eir bands mobile app, you can target specific muscle groups, choose from over 20 training, recovery, and pain-management modes, and adjust intensity to match your goals. Whether you’re building strength, easing soreness, or managing everyday muscle discomfort, eir bands adapt to how your body moves.
For next-level training, switch to EIR Training, our flagship VR fitness experience. Stimulation syncs with your virtual movements, delivering real-time muscle activation across immersive workouts — helping you train harder while recovering smarter.

EMS muscle stimulation for strength training
