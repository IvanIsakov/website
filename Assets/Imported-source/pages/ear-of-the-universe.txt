EAR OF THE UNIVERSE
Audio-visuo-tactile installation for Nowhere festival
A multisensory participatory experience which is simultaneously a new kind of spatial musical instrument allowing the audience to play a desert symphony with the rope art piece. The audience will feel the telekinesis-like power, transforming movements and space into the audiovisual sacred dance. Was showcased at Nowhere 2019 in Spain and London decompression 2019.
A collaboration between Katerina Loschinina, Ivan Isakov, Andrey Novikov, Nik Nightingale, Ilya Batyi and Oleg Gergel
