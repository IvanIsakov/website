A VR fitness game for Meta Quest headsets

EIR Training - is a high intensity virtual reality fitness app, utilising muscle haptic armbands - eir bands . It delivers combination of cardio and muscle training in an engaging gamified fashion. It was built in Unity3D in 2022 and overhauled in 2025 by a team lead by Ivan, with developers Matthew Jenkins, Sybille Rerolle, Dan Tonks, and designers Sanofer Hyderali & Decca Chan.

Additionally, Eir training was adapted as a competitive fitness experience for conventions in 2024 as a project commissioned by ASICS. It saw succesful demonstrations at the Sushi Tech Tokyo conference, and in the ASICS house during Paris Olympics in 2024.
