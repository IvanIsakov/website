A non-visual experience taking you on a journey of introspection.

___________________________________________________

Olfactory Circles is a mutlimodal olfactory experience (olfactus meaning "the sense of smell") created by multimedia artists, designers, singers & performers: Ivan Isakov, Inessa Lomas, Andrey Novikov and Yana Vlaeva.

Custom fragrances created for this experience take you on a round trip through space, time, nature, culture, rituals and earth, evoking long lost memories and crafting new visions.

The scents are aided by soundscapes, ancient poetry and folk music.

What do you feel, what do you hear, what do you see - within you?
