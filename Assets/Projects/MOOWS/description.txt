A zine by, for, and about the cows.
CowZine is a small printed-and-stapled creature put together by the MoonCows, and anyone else hanging around the herd. It's part-newspaper, part-art-mag, part-yearbook, part-classifieds. There's no theme, no party line, — just a deadline and a pile of pages waiting to be filled.

Idea and Editorial committee - Ivan & Bennie.
