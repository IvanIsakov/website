What if virtual reality could help stroke patients recover faster — and make therapy more engaging?

In this episode of The Sync, host Allan Rankin sits down with Ivan Isakov, Co-Founder & CEO of Valkyrie Industries, to talk about how VR is transforming rehabilitation for people with neurological conditions.

Ivan shares the story behind the founding of Valkyrie Industries, how immersive VR experiences can trick the brain into relearning movement, and why an intern’s idea changed the company’s entire direction.

From startup challenges to breakthroughs in neuroscience and physical therapy, this conversation explores how technology and empathy are coming together to redefine recovery.
