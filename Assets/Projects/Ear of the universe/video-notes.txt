Ear Of The Universe @ Nowhere 2019
Video film of Ear of the Universe installation @ Nowhere 2019 Festival.

“EAR OF THE UNIVERSE” is a multi-sensory participatory experience which is simultaneously a new kind of spatial musical instrument allowing the audience to play a desert symphony with the rope art piece. The audience will feel the telekinesis-like power, transforming movements and space into the audiovisual sacred dance.

By Katerina Loschinina, Ivan Isakov, Andrey Novikov, Nik Nightingale, Ilia Batii and Oleg Gergel
