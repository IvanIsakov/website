Gamified | Interactive | Virtual FES Therapy
v-health XR, our advanced muscle recovery platform, combines patented Functional Electrical Muscle Stimulation (FES) technology with a gamified user experience, supporting individuals in rebuilding muscle strength, mobility, and motor control. 

With real-time motion stimulation in extended reality (XR) and intuitive app control, it’s designed to make muscle recovery engaging and adaptable. From helping with upper limb injuries to enhancing muscle re-education, v-health XR provides seamless, interactive experiences that empower users to take an active role in their recovery.
