Smart | Wireless | Customised FES Therapy
Designed for maximum customisation and simplicity, v-health Mobile helps individuals strengthen muscles, reduce atrophy, and relearn everyday movements. By combining our CE-marked, patented Functional Electrical Stimulation (FES) wearable, eir bands, with a companion mobile app, it brings clinically-supported, wireless, and gamified recovery directly into the hands of clinicians and patients.

It was built in 2024-25 by team led by Ivan Isakov, developers Dan Tonks, Matthew Jenkins, designers Sanofer Hyderali & Decca Chan.
