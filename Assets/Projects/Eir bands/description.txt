Wearable Muscle Stimulation for neuromuscular health

eir bands use electrical impulses to stimulate targeted muscles, providing a responsive sensations that reacts to every movement within digital environments. As muscles interact with virtual surroundings, the bands adapt in real-time to deliver motion stimulation to those muscles. 

This interactive approach aids muscle training by activating and strengthening muscles during immersive sessions, promoting muscle health, endurance, and recovery in a personalised, engaging way. eir bands were proven to improve muscle metabolism. 

Under my leadership, between 2022 and 2025 Valkyrie Industries have designed and manufactured eir bands for several markets: virtual fitness, neurorehabilitation, muscle recovery. Devices have been manufactured by Luxshare & Cosmo.
