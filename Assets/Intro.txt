ABOUT
A scientist turned entrepreneur. I worked on ZnO nanowires during my PhD at UCL and on cheap high-performance semiconductors during my time at Imperial College London as a postdoc. In 2017, after my lab was disbanded, together with Kourosh Atefipour and Oliver Cleveley-Jones I co-founded Valkyrie Industries - a VR haptics startup. In parallel, I have been working on multiple technical and creative projects, both physical and virtual. Since 2026, I am on sabbatical in NYC, exploring the world of immediate software, new types of human expression and physical AI.
​

My main interests include interactive design, wearables, research-based technology, and user-centered problem-solving. I manage various multidisciplinary teams to deliver broad range medical, wellness, 3D, hardware and software projects, some of them are presented on this website. I am an educator, an international speaker, community builder and an artist.
